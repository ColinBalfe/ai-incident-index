What the level means

The level is the highest score among AI incidents disclosed in the last 24 hours and the incident being tracked live, by the index’s published formula: WS-1 Minor, WS-2 Moderate, WS-3 Serious, WS-4 Severe, WS-5 Critical. Every incident behind it links to its sources.

Use it freely. Please keep the link so readers can see why the level is what it is. Writing about AI incidents? See the press page.
