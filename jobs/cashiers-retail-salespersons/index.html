Will AI replace cashiers and retail workers? The evidence

Reviewed by Colin Balfe on Oct 27, 2026 · Updated Oct 27, 2026 · Graded, dated evidence, not predictions
The short answer

Self-checkout and online shopping, not chatbots, are what BLS expects to shrink cashier jobs, by about 200,000 by 2035. AI shopping assistants now handle product questions online at huge scale, but camera-based 'just walk out' stores and unattended checkouts have hit limits, and several chains have pulled back self-checkout. Walmart says AI will change every job but plans to keep headcount flat.

The job today

Cashiers: 3,106,300; retail salespersons: 3,998,100 (2025)people employed in the USBLS Occupational Outlook Handbook

What to do

  1. Move toward service rolesCashier jobs are shrinking with self-checkout and online sales. Customer help, returns, pickup and department roles hold up better.
  2. Become the self-checkout attendantRetailers that kept self-checkout still need staff to oversee it, fix errors and prevent loss.
  3. Learn the store's techHandheld devices, pickup systems and inventory tools are where retailers say the job mix is shifting.
  4. Build product expertiseSpecialist advice in areas like electronics, home improvement or cosmetics is harder to replace than checkout.

Questions people ask

Will AI replace cashiers?

Self-checkout and online shopping are shrinking the job more than AI is. BLS projects a 6% decline (-200,600 jobs) for 2025-35.

Are retail salespersons at risk?

BLS projects roughly flat employment (-14,000 jobs) for 2025-35, citing e-commerce competition. Its page does not mention AI.

Are cashierless stores taking over?

Not yet. Amazon removed its camera-based Just Walk Out system from US Amazon Fresh stores in 2024, and several chains have pulled back self-checkout.

Is Walmart cutting jobs for AI?

Walmart's CEO says AI will change every job, but the company plans to keep its workforce at about 2.1 million for three years.

How we grade

Each task is graded 'deployed' only when a named organisation reports AI doing it in real work at scale, 'demonstrated' when it works in tests, benchmarks or pilots, and 'not yet' when we found no credible evidence AI does it reliably. For job losses we separate what a company said in its own words ('company says AI') from what reporters or analysts attributed to AI ('reported as AI') and from research ('study'); employment and outlook figures come from the BLS 2025-35 projections.
