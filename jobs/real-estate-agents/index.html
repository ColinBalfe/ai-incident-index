Will AI replace real estate agents? What the evidence shows

Reviewed by Colin Balfe on Oct 27, 2026 · Updated Oct 27, 2026 · Graded, dated evidence, not predictions
The short answer

About two-thirds of Realtors use AI, mostly to write listing copy and marketing, and a few startups now sell AI-led home buying with licensed agents in the background. Machines have struggled with the core judgment: Zillow quit algorithmic home buying in 2021. Agent numbers are falling, but because of a frozen housing market and costs, not AI.

Jobs evidence so far

Labelled by who says AI was the reason: the company itself, reporters, or a study.

  • 2026-05-31 NAR membership is projected to fall to about 1.2 million in 2026 from a pandemic peak of 1.6 million, blamed on a stalled housing market and agent costs; 76% of agents expect more competition from tech and AI (not attributed to AI) (Propmodo, May 2026)
  • 2025-09-18 NAR survey: 46% of agents report no noticeable impact from AI on their business, and 50% a positive one (study) (NAR, Sep 2025)
  • 2021-11-02 Zillow closes its algorithm-driven home-buying arm and cuts 25% of staff, citing difficulty forecasting prices (company statement; a failed automation bet, not AI replacing agents) (CNBC, Nov 2021)

What to do

  1. Let AI write the first draftListing copy, social posts and market summaries are where most agents already use AI. Use it, then check facts about the property.
  2. Compete on local knowledge and negotiationPricing calls, showings and negotiation are where even AI-first brokerages still rely on licensed people.
  3. Watch the AI-led brokeragesLow-commission AI buying services exist in some states. Know what they offer so you can explain your value to clients.
  4. Manage costs through the downturnAgent numbers are falling mainly because of the housing slump and fees, so financial resilience matters more than AI right now.

Questions people ask

Will AI replace real estate agents?

Not on current evidence. BLS projects 2% growth for 2025-35 and does not mention AI. AI-led buying services still use licensed humans.

Why are agents leaving the business?

Reporting ties the drop to the slowest housing market in decades and agents' fixed costs, not AI.

Can AI price a home?

Automated estimates exist, but Zillow shut its algorithm-driven home-buying business in 2021 after finding prices far harder to forecast than expected.

How do agents use AI today?

Mostly for content: 46% use AI-generated content such as listing descriptions, according to NAR's 2025 survey.

How we grade

Each task is graded 'deployed' only when a named organisation reports AI doing it in real work at scale, 'demonstrated' when it works in tests, benchmarks or pilots, and 'not yet' when we found no credible evidence AI does it reliably. For job losses we separate what a company said in its own words ('company says AI') from what reporters or analysts attributed to AI ('reported as AI') and from research ('study'); employment and outlook figures come from the BLS 2025-35 projections.
