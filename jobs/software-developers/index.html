Can AI do your job? · Software developers

Will AI replace software developers? The evidence

Reviewed by Colin Balfe on Oct 27, 2026 · Updated Oct 27, 2026 · Graded, dated evidence, not predictions
The short answer

AI now writes a large share of new code at some big companies, and coding is the single biggest use of AI chat tools. Measured productivity gains for experienced developers are smaller and less certain than the claims, and the clearest job effect so far is fewer openings for young developers rather than mass layoffs of experienced ones.

Jobs evidence so far

Labelled by who says AI was the reason: the company itself, reporters, or a study.

  • 2026-02-26 Block cuts about 4,000 of 10,000+ staff (40%); Jack Dorsey wrote 'Intelligence tools have changed what it means to build and run a company' (company says AI; roles cut not specified) (SF Standard, Feb 2026)
  • 2026-08-12 Stanford/ADP: employment of 22-25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations is 19% below trend, mainly through reduced hiring; the pattern holds excluding tech firms (study) (Stanford Digital Economy Lab, Aug 2026)
  • 2026-07-11 Indeed: US software development postings up almost 15% since Claude Code launched but still about 27.5% below pre-pandemic levels (study) (OfficeChai on Indeed Hiring Lab, Jul 2026)
  • 2026-10-26 Microsoft offers voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees, framed by press as an AI-era overhaul; memo excerpts do not mention AI (reported as AI) (TechCrunch, Oct 2026)

What to do

  1. Get good at directing and reviewing agentsWriting specs, breaking work into tasks and reviewing AI-written code is becoming the core skill at companies where most new code is AI-generated.
  2. Own the parts benchmarks skipRequirements, architecture, security, production incidents and trade-off decisions are where ambiguity lives and tools are weakest.
  3. Measure your own speedupMETR found developers believed AI sped them up even when it slowed them down; time real tasks with and without tools.
  4. Watch junior hiring, not headlinesThe clearest signal so far is fewer entry-level hires; early-career developers should build visible, shipped work.

Questions people ask

Will AI replace software developers?

Not on current evidence. BLS projects 10% growth to 2035, and Indeed postings rose in 2025-26 though they remain well below pre-pandemic levels. AI is changing the work rather than removing the role.

Is it true AI writes most code at Google?

Google's CEO said so in Apr 2026 (75% of new code). That is a company claim; how much human review that code gets was not detailed.

Does AI actually make developers faster?

Unclear. A 2025 randomized trial found experienced developers 19% slower; a 2026 follow-up suggested modest speedups but METR called its own data unreliable.

Are junior developers hit hardest?

Stanford's payroll study finds young workers in AI-exposed jobs 19% below trend, mainly through fewer hires. The authors call this descriptive, not proof that AI caused it.

Which developer tasks are safest?

Defining requirements, system design, security and incident response, and accountability for what ships.

How we grade

Each task is graded 'deployed' only when a named organisation reports AI doing it in real work at scale, 'demonstrated' when it works in tests, benchmarks or pilots, and 'not yet' when we found no credible evidence AI does it reliably. For job losses we separate what a company said in its own words ('company says AI') from what reporters or analysts attributed to AI ('reported as AI') and from research ('study'); employment and outlook figures come from the BLS 2025-35 projections.
