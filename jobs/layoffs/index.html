Layoffs tied to AI: the ledger

Every job cut on our record where AI is part of the story, newest first, labelled by who says AI was the reason.

Our investigation: October's “AI layoffs”, checked: in none of the five is AI clearly doing the work →

How to read it

Company says AI: the employer itself tied the cuts to AI. Reported as AI: reporters or analysts made the link; the company didn't. A cut that names no AI link isn't evidence that AI took the jobs, and we label it that way.
