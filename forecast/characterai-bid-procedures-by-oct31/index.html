Why the index thinks so

31 states and DC filed a 27-page objection on Oct 23 (Docket 66) asking the court to deny or cut the 3.5% fee, lower the $21.7 million overbid floor and give bidders 21 more days. Break-up fees for stalking-horse bidders are routinely approved in Delaware, but a coordinated objection from most state attorneys general in a case about harm to minors is not routine, and courts often trim timelines rather than fees.

What would move it

The Oct 30 10 a.m. ET hearing docket; a revised bidding-procedures order; Disney amending its terms before the hearing.
