# Glossary bank

- **AI agent:** an AI that can take actions, such as sending, buying, logging in or running code, not just chat.
- **Jailbreak:** a prompt designed to make an AI ignore its own safety rules.
- **Bug bounty:** a scheme where a company pays outsiders to find holes in its systems and report them, under agreed rules.
- **PHP:** the software that runs most of the world's websites.
- **Patch (fix):** an update that closes a known hole. "Unpatched" means still unfixed.
- **Honeypot (decoy server):** a computer left open on purpose to watch how attackers behave.
- **Model weights:** the AI itself: the billions of numbers that make it work.
- **Deepfake:** fake video, audio or images of a real person, made with AI.
- **Voice clone:** an AI copy of someone's voice, made from a few seconds of recordings.
- **Open-weight model:** an AI whose weights are published, so anyone can download and run it, with or without its safety settings.
- **America's cyber agency (CISA):** the US government agency that warns organizations about hacks. Its formal warning is a listing in its catalogue of flaws known to be used in real attacks.
- **Tried / Proven / Hit:** Tried means attacks are being attempted. Proven means a test system actually fell. Hit means a real victim, confirmed.
- **WS level:** the highest score among AI incidents in the last 24 hours and the story we're tracking live, on a published 0–100 formula: WS-1 Minor up to WS-5 Critical.
