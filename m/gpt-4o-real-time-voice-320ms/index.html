GPT-4o answers by voice in 320 ms, conversation speed, free to all

OpenAI

320 msAverage voice response latency

GPT-4o responded to audio in as little as 232 ms (320 ms average), down from 2.8-5.4 s in the previous Voice Mode, and became the free-tier ChatGPT model; METR 50% horizon about 7 minutes.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.
