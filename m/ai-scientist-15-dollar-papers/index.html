The AI Scientist writes full ML papers end to end for about $15 each

Sakana AI

about $15Cost per generated paper

Sakana's AI Scientist generated ideas, ran experiments, wrote LaTeX papers and reviewed them in an open-ended loop at roughly $15 per paper; its best outputs were rated 'Weak Accept' by its own automated reviewer.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.

What this could change for you

Scientific papers can now be mass-produced by software, which puts the burden on reviewers and readers to tell real findings from plausible filler.
