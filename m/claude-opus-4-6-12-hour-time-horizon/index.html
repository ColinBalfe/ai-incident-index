Claude Opus 4.6: METR 50% time horizon reaches 12 hours

Anthropic

12 hoursMETR 50% time horizon

METR measured Claude Opus 4.6's 50% time horizon at 718.8 minutes (about 12 hours, CI 5.3-60 h), up from 4.9 h for Opus 4.5; it scored 81.4% on SWE-bench Verified and shipped 'agent teams' in Claude Code that coordinate autonomously.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.

What this could change for you

An agent that can work a full day unattended is what makes 'AI employee' products plausible, and what makes one wrong command catastrophic.
