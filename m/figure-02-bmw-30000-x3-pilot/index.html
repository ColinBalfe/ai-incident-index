Humanoids on a BMW line: 1,250 hours, 90,000 parts, 30,000 X3s built

Figure AI / BMW Group

30,000+ BMW X3Vehicles supported during the pilot

In an 11-month pilot at BMW's Spartanburg plant, Figure 02 humanoids worked 10-hour shifts loading more than 90,000 sheet-metal parts at over 99% placement accuracy on an 84-second cycle, supporting production of more than 30,000 BMW X3 vehicles.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.
