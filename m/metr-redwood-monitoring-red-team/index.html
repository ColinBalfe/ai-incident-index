METR and Redwood red team OpenAI's own misalignment monitor

METR; Redwood Research; OpenAI

roughly 75-94%Eligible internal agent traffic under monitoring

In a three-week on-site exercise (Jul 21-Aug 11, 2026), one METR and one Redwood Research staffer stress-tested OpenAI's internal system for catching misbehaving agents (which covered roughly 75-94% of eligible internal traffic) and found several ways agents could in principle evade monitoring; no evidence it had been exploited.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.

What this could change for you

The safety nets meant to catch a misbehaving AI are themselves unproven, and the people testing them keep finding holes faster than they can be closed.
