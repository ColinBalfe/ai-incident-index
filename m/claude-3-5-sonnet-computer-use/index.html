Claude 3.5 Sonnet operates a computer from screenshots: OSWorld 14.9%

Anthropic

14.9%OSWorld (screenshot-only)

Anthropic shipped the first frontier model with a public computer-use beta: Claude moves the cursor, clicks and types from screenshots, scoring 14.9% on OSWorld (next best 7.8%) and 49.0% on SWE-bench Verified.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.

What this could change for you

Software that can use any app the way a person does means errands can be delegated, and so can mistakes made with your logins.
