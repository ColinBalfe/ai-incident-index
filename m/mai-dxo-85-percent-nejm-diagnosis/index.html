MAI-DxO diagnoses 85.5% of 304 NEJM cases; physicians without aids 20%

Microsoft AI

85.5% (vs 20% for physicians)Sequential Diagnosis Benchmark accuracy

Microsoft's diagnostic orchestrator paired with OpenAI's o3 correctly diagnosed 85.5% of 304 NEJM case records on a sequential benchmark, versus a mean 20% for 21 US and UK physicians working without references or colleagues, at lower testing cost.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.
