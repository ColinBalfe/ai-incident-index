AI copilot cuts diagnostic errors 16% across 39,849 clinic visits in Kenya

OpenAI / Penda Health

16%Relative reduction in diagnostic errors

In Penda Health's Nairobi primary-care clinics, clinicians randomly given a GPT-4o-based 'AI Consult' made 16% fewer diagnostic errors and 13% fewer treatment errors across 39,849 visits, judged from 5,666 randomly selected charts rated by 108 physicians.

How far it has travelled

From a demonstration to real life, in five steps. Empty steps are the point: a demonstration is not a deployment.

What this could change for you

In clinics short of doctors, an AI second opinion already changes what patients are diagnosed with, though it has not yet been shown to make them feel better.
