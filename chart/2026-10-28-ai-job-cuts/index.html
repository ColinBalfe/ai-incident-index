October already has 6 AI-linked job cuts on our ledger, more than any other month this past year

Our ledger holds 7 AI-linked job-cut announcements from the last three months against 6 in the nine months before; in 7 of 13 the company itself named AI.

Why it matters

Layoff headlines blur who is actually blaming AI. This separates cuts the employer tied to AI from cuts only reporters linked to it, so the trend can be read honestly. Part of the recent rise is that we started collecting in earnest this autumn, so read the red share as much as the height.
