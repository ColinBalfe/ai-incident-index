AI went from tool to actor: 30 incidents this year where AI acted on its own

In 2023 and 2024, no real-world AI incident on the record had the AI acting on its own. This year it happened in 30 of 69.

Why it matters

The question people ask is whether AI is just a tool. The record says it was, until about a year ago. Part of the rise is that more incidents get reported now, so look at the share: in 2023 and 2024 the AI never acted on its own; this year it did in 43% of cases.
