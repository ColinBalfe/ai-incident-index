Stanford Internet Observatory found many hundreds of known child sexual abuse images, plus further candidates later verified by child-safety groups, in the LAION-5B image-text dataset. Portions of the dataset had been used to train Stable Diffusion models.

Damage

hundreds of known CSAM items embedded in a widely used open training set; downstream effects on models unquantified

Sources

Stanford. Dataset withdrawal by LAION was widely reported but not verified here.
