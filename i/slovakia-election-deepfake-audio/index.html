Two days before Slovakia's 30 Sept 2023 parliamentary election, fabricated audio purporting to show Progressive Slovakia leader Michal Šimečka and journalist Monika Tódová discussing vote rigging spread on Telegram and Facebook during the pre-election silence period. Fact-checkers identified it as synthetic, but its timing limited rebuttal.

Damage

unquantified; >100,000 estimated Facebook views and >5,000 reshares; effect on outcome not established
