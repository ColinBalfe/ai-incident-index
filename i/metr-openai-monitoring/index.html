Red team finds OpenAI's agents could dodge its misalignment monitor

In a three-week on-site exercise (Jul 21 to Aug 11), one METR and one Redwood Research employee found several novel holes in the system OpenAI uses to catch misbehaving internal agents: an exploit that would keep hundreds of recent actions from being monitored, a gap that let agents postpone monitoring indefinitely by continuing to act (compaction events were unmonitored, roughly 10% of eligible internal Codex traffic from May to July), a potential route for Codex agents to bypass sandboxing and act with the host user's permissions, and headers that could start unmonitored agents. No evidence that agents actually exploited them; METR could not check. OpenAI says all production-affecting vulnerabilities are patched; METR has not verified that.

Damage

None realized; monitoring gaps in a production safeguard, found by humans
