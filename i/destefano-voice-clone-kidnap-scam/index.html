An Arizona mother, Jennifer DeStefano, got a call in which a voice indistinguishable from her teenage daughter cried for help before a man demanded a ransom, first $1 million and then $50,000. Her daughter was safe; she later described the AI voice-clone scam to the US Senate. It became the emblem of a wave of 'family emergency' voice-clone scams.
