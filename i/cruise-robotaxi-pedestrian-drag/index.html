After a human-driven car knocked a pedestrian into its path in San Francisco, a driverless Cruise vehicle stopped on top of her and then executed a pull-over maneuver, dragging her about 20 feet. California's DMV suspended Cruise's driverless permits on 24 Oct 2023, alleging it had not initially disclosed the dragging; Cruise later paid federal penalties for incomplete and false reporting.

Damage

1 pedestrian seriously injured; $1.5M NHTSA civil penalty (Sept 2024); $500,000 DOJ criminal fine for false report; Cruise driverless operations halted nationwide
