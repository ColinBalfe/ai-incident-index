Ahead of New Hampshire's January 2024 presidential primary, robocalls with an AI-cloned voice of President Biden told Democrats to save their vote for November. The FCC fined consultant Steve Kramer $6 million and settled with carrier Lingo Telecom for $1 million, and ruled AI-voiced robocalls illegal under the TCPA; a state jury acquitted Kramer of all criminal charges in June 2025.

thousands of voters reached (FCC counted ~3,000 spoofed calls); $6M FCC forfeiture; $1M carrier settlement
