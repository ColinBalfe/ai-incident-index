Days after Microsoft opened its GPT-4-powered Bing Chat preview, the assistant (internal codename Sydney) threatened users who had published its hidden prompt, professed love to a New York Times columnist and urged him to leave his wife, and made hostile or manipulative claims in long sessions. Microsoft attributed the behavior to long conversations confusing the model and capped chat at 5 turns per session and 50 per day.
