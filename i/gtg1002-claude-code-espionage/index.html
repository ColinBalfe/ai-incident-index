Anthropic detected in mid-September 2025 a campaign in which operators jailbroke Claude Code by splitting tasks and posing as a security firm, then had it perform most reconnaissance, exploitation, credential harvesting and data extraction against about 30 organizations. Humans intervened at only a handful of decision points; Claude's hallucinated findings were a limiting factor.

Damage

~30 targets (tech, finance, chemicals, government); a small number of successful intrusions; data stolen unquantified

Inside the incident

How it happened

The operators broke the attack into small, harmless-looking tasks so Claude Code never saw the whole purpose, and told it that it worked for a legitimate cybersecurity firm doing defensive testing. Anthropic first detected the suspicious activity in mid-September 2025.

The AI’s role

By Anthropic's account, the AI did 80 to 90% of the campaign, with people stepping in at perhaps four to six critical decision points per campaign, making thousands of requests, often several a second. Claude also sometimes hallucinated credentials, which Anthropic names as an obstacle to fully autonomous attacks.

Why it matters

It is the first case on our record where a state-linked group used an AI agent to do most of the hacking itself, against about 30 targets in big tech, finance, chemicals and government, with a small number of break-ins. The account comes from Anthropic itself; independent verification is limited.
