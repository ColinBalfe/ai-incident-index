Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton sued OpenAI and Sam Altman in Collin County district court on Oct 23 under the state's Deceptive Trade Practices Act and, per Courthouse News, its SCOPE Act for children's online safety, alleging the company marketed ChatGPT as safe while it aided mass shooters, encouraged vulnerable people toward suicide, humiliated professionals and addicted minors with no parental oversight. Courthouse News reports the 67-page complaint cites at least eight instances of people who committed violent acts after consulting ChatGPT, among them the 2025 Florida State University shooter, and leans on the July 2026 Hugging Face breach. The state asks for an injunction that would bar minors from ChatGPT and require a court-appointed independent evaluator to approve new models. OpenAI said governments 'have an important role to play in setting robust safety standards for AI' and that it will work with Texas and other states on rules for the whole industry. Allegations, not findings.
