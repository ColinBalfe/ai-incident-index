Air Canada held liable for its chatbot's bad advice

Air Canada's website chatbot told a grieving customer he could claim a bereavement fare retroactively, contrary to actual policy. British Columbia's Civil Resolution Tribunal rejected the airline's argument that the chatbot was a separate entity responsible for its own statements and ordered compensation.

Damage

C$812.02 ordered (C$650.88 damages plus interest and fees)

Sources

Civilresolutionbc, second source. Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149. Date is the ruling; the chatbot conversation took place in Nov 2022 (before the window).
