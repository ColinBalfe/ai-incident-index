Police in Pennsylvania allege that Robert Mayberry, 41, photographed a teenage girl at West Goshen Community Park and used Grok to turn the image into child sexual abuse material, according to NBC10 Philadelphia as relayed by Tech Times UK on Oct 26. Investigators report finding hundreds of AI-generated images and videos on seized devices, along with more than 150 videos allegedly filmed beneath the clothing of women and teenage girls. Mayberry was arrested on Oct 21 after an investigation that began on June 18, faces charges including sexual abuse of children, invasion of privacy and criminal use of a communication facility, has posted bail and has a preliminary hearing set for Oct 30. He was placed on leave from his job at a King of Prussia Mall store; his attorney had not responded to requests for comment. The charges are allegations.
