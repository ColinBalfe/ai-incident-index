Deloitte refunds Australia for report with AI-invented sources

A 237-page Deloitte review for Australia's employment department contained a fabricated quote attributed to a federal court judgment and references to non-existent academic works. After University of Sydney researcher Chris Rudge flagged up to 20 errors, Deloitte reissued it disclosing Azure OpenAI use and agreed to repay the final contract instalment.
