By October 11, 220 AI-generated political ads backed by $137 million had run, including fabricated statements and depictions of candidates in several Senate and governor races. Texas saw the first criminal complaint under its election-deepfake law, and the FBI and CISA warned officials of AI-enhanced phishing; Russian and Iranian networks pushed AI video.
