PHP2Shell: AI-found PHP flaws, global precautionary shutdowns

The fix exists for supported PHP versions and nobody has broken it yet; the US government lists the flaws as exploited, one security firm reports 25 real servers rooted from the attacker's own logs, but no organization has confirmed a breach by name and most exposed servers will never get the patch.

70/100
WS-4 Severe
Incident WS-0153, Oct 21, 2026: PHP2Shell: AI-found PHP flaws leak, trigger global shutdowns

The chain

The report, the responsible party’s response, the fix they claimed, whether anyone independent checked, and where it stands. A company saying it fixed something and evidence that the fix worked are different rows.

  1. Reported Aug 11, 2026

    Calif, working with OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol under OpenAI's 'Patch the Planet' program, reported the flaw to the PHP security team on Aug 11; Wiz independently found it with AI assistance (Aug 19) and reported it Aug 24. PHP acknowledged and began a coordinated fix Sep 21. The CVE IDs, a proof-of-concept video and a pseudonymous forum post leaked on Oct 20, a day before the planned release. source

  2. Response Oct 21, 2026

    The PHP Foundation moved the release up a day, shipped fixes on Oct 21 (~14:00 UTC), and publicly blamed the leak; it said Chainguard 'unilaterally broke the embargo' by publishing fixed images six hours early (Chainguard disputes this). It acknowledged the underlying issue had surfaced in 2020 and in a 2024 audit, called the delay a structural failure and asked companies and governments for sustained security funding. CISA, NSA, the UK NCSC and other national agencies issued same-day patch-or-go-offline advisories. source

  3. Claimed fix Oct 23, 2026 partial

    Fixed releases PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 address CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619; branches before 8.2 get no official fix (Qualys counts about 110 million instances, 71%, on end-of-life versions; Censys still counts 34.4 million vulnerable services). On Oct 23 PHP flagged a third, narrower code-execution bug (CVE-2026-115560) and said it will fix it in the next regular release because 'the issue can only occur under an unusual combination of configuration and application code'; the interim advice is to disable session upload progress. source

  4. Independent check Oct 24, 2026 partial

    No independent party has reported a way around the Oct 21 patch. VulnCheck says it has a working exploit against the recommended production configuration of unpatched hosts, kept to paying customers, and 'We are not aware of any other public or commercial exploit that achieves RCE on multiple versions of PHP.' Hunt.io, Wordfence, SOCRadar and Sansec report millions of attempts but no confirmed compromise; Sophos corrected its one claim of a successful exploit. Nobody has published an independent review of the patch's completeness, and CISA added two of the three CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list on Oct 26, which confirms exploitation but names no victim. Oct 24: CISA's catalog, opened directly, still lists neither CVE (version 2026.10.23). source

  5. Where it stands Oct 27, 2026 ongoing

    Day seven after the patch. America's cyber agency (CISA) added CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Oct 26 with a federal fix deadline of Oct 29; it named no victim. The security firm Dream reported on Oct 26 that attacker files it recovered show a chained PHP2Shell and Linux-kernel exploit rooting 25 real servers (24 firewalls and a management server) on Oct 23-24, three in a US government-affiliated network (The Hacker News, Oct 28); no victim or vendor is named and none has confirmed it, so this is a single-firm report. Wiz still sees only 'a limited number of exploitation attempts' in its own telemetry. No named organization has confirmed a breach, and the third PHP bug (CVE-2026-115560) has no fix. source

What would change the verdict

A confirmed compromise at a named organization, or a public way around the Oct 21 patch, would turn precautionary disruption into real damage. (CISA's Oct 26 listing confirmed exploitation, but no victim.)

Method

Statuses move only on evidence: fixed needs an independent party confirming the fix under stated conditions; unverified is a claimed fix nobody independent has checked; disputed, reopened (recurred after a claimed fix), unresolved (no fix claimed), ongoing (still unfolding). “No public response found” is a valid entry; nothing here is invented.
