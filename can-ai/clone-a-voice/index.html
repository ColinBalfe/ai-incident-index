Can AI clone someone's voice from a short clip?

Reviewed by Colin Balfe on Oct 27, 2026 · Updated Oct 27, 2026 · Graded on a five-step evidence ladder
Yes
The answer

Yes. A few seconds to a few minutes of someone's recorded speech is enough for widely available tools to produce a convincing copy of their voice.

First shown: Jan 5, 2023. Microsoft researchers' VALL-E produced speech in the voice of an unseen speaker from a 3-second recording. (VALL-E paper (arXiv))

What it means

Voice cloning means software learns how one person sounds (pitch, accent, rhythm) from a recording and then says anything you type in that voice. In January 2023 Microsoft's VALL-E research system did this from a 3-second sample, without having been trained on that speaker.

Within weeks it left the lab. ElevenLabs opened a public beta in January 2023 that cloned voices from short snippets, and had more than a million registered users by June 2023. In February 2023 a journalist used its free service, with about five minutes of his own speech, to get past his bank's voice check.

The limits are about quality, not possibility. Short clips give a rougher copy than long ones, unusual accents are harder, and a live back-and-forth is harder to fake than a pre-written message. But a frightened parent hearing a crying child on a bad line is not listening carefully, which is why scammers use it.

Safeguards are thin. In March 2025 Consumer Reports found that four of six popular voice-cloning services failed to take basic steps to stop people cloning a voice without its owner's consent.

What it means for you

A voice on the phone is no longer proof of who is speaking, even someone you know well. Any public video, voicemail or social media clip can be enough raw material.

What to do

  1. Agree a family code wordPick a word or question only your family knows and ask for it on any urgent call about money or danger.
  2. Hang up and call backIf a call asks for money urgently, end it and ring the person on the number you already have.
  3. Distrust urgency and secrecyPressure to act now and tell no one is the scam's engine, whatever the voice sounds like.
  4. Drop voice-only bank loginIf your bank lets your voice act as a password, ask for a stronger option such as an app or PIN.

Questions people ask

How much audio does AI need to clone a voice?

Research systems have worked from 3 seconds; consumer tools ask for a short clip, and a few minutes of audio gives a closer match.

Can I tell a cloned voice from a real one?

Not reliably by ear, especially on a phone line and under stress. Verify by calling back or asking a code word instead.

Do voice-cloning companies stop misuse?

Some do more than others. Consumer Reports found in March 2025 that four of six popular services lacked basic steps to confirm the speaker consented.

Is using a cloned voice to trick people illegal?

Using it to defraud someone is fraud. In the US, AI-voiced robocalls were ruled illegal under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act in 2024.
